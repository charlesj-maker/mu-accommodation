MU Accommodation Privacy Policy

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 28 July 2026

MU Accommodation ("the App", "we", "us") helps students find verified boarding houses near Mulungushi University, and helps landlords list their properties. This policy explains what information we collect, why, and how it's handled — for both the mobile app and the website at muaccommodation.site.

1. Information We Collect

Account information. When you sign up, we collect your email address and a password (handled securely by Firebase Authentication — we never see or store your raw password).

Payment information. To unlock a landlord's contact details and exact location, students pay a one-time access fee via mobile money (Airtel Money or MTN MoMo). We collect the mobile money number you're paying from, and — for MTN — the account name registered to that number, so the payment can be automatically matched and confirmed. We never collect mobile money PINs, card numbers, or access to your mobile money account itself — payment happens entirely within your own mobile money app, outside of ours.

Listing information. Landlords who upload a boarding house provide the property name, description, price, room availability, contact number, WhatsApp number, photos, and a map location for the listing.

Location data. When a landlord pins a property's location using the in-app map picker, the app may request access to device location to help center the map. Students do not have their live location collected — only the landlord-submitted address/pin for each listing.

Communications. If you contact us for support (e.g. via WhatsApp), we may keep a record of that conversation to help resolve your query.

Basic device/usage data. Like most apps, we use Firebase's standard infrastructure, which may log basic technical data (such as app version, crash reports, and general usage patterns) to help us keep the app working reliably.

2. How We Use Information

  • To create and manage your account
  • To display verified boarding house listings to students
  • To confirm mobile money payments and unlock the relevant listing's contact details
  • To let students reach landlords directly via WhatsApp, call, or map location once unlocked
  • To respond to support requests, refund requests, or reports of inaccurate listings
  • To keep the platform secure and prevent abuse

3. How Payments Work

Access-fee payments are sent directly to the app owner's mobile money account. A separate automated process reads the payment confirmation message from that mobile money account and matches it to your pending payment (by phone number for Airtel, or by account name for MTN), then unlocks the listing. We do not use a third-party payment processor that stores your financial details, and the app itself never has access to your mobile money account balance or PIN.

4. Sharing of Information

We do not sell your personal information. Information is shared only in these limited ways:

  • With landlords: once you unlock a listing, you can see and contact that landlord's published phone/WhatsApp number, and they may receive your message directly through WhatsApp.
  • With our infrastructure providers: data is stored using Google Firebase (Firestore, Authentication), and parts of our backend run on Vercel. These providers process data on our behalf under their own security and privacy commitments.
  • If required by law, or to protect the safety of our users or the integrity of the platform.

5. Data Storage and Security

Data is stored using Google Firebase (Cloud Firestore), with access rules restricting who can read or write which data — for example, a student can only view their own pending payment records, and listings can only be edited through the app's landlord/admin flow. All connections use HTTPS encryption.

6. Data Retention

We keep account and listing data for as long as your account is active, or as needed to provide the service. Payment records are kept for reasonable record-keeping and dispute-resolution purposes. You can request deletion of your account and associated data at any time (see Section 8).

7. Children's Privacy

MU Accommodation is intended for university students and is not directed at children under 16. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 16.

8. Your Rights & Account Deletion

You can request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal data at any time by contacting us using the details below. We will respond to reasonable requests within a reasonable timeframe.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time as the app evolves. Material changes will be reflected on this page with an updated "Last updated" date.
